What the reproduction shows

  • Liquefier sheets are true optima. Starting the optimiser elsewhere, it lands on the book's turbine discharge temperatures for both Fig. 3.61 cycles (to 0.01 K), and on the Table 3.8 flows (15.64 and 7.76 g/s).
  • Refrigerator sheets are not at this model's optimum. With the same equations that reproduce the book's own points, the parallel cycle reaches 12.55 g/s (book 12.60, 0.4 % lower), the LN2 refrigerator 8.05 g/s (book 9.94) and the refrigerator without LN2 9.16 g/s (Table 3.8: 10.81). All flows stay positive and every HX keeps its end-temperature differences. The book does not state the extra constraint its solver used, so treat the book's refrigerator figures as conservative, not as optima.
  • The series sheet is unconverged. Its printed Brayton 1 balance is +10.18 W (model at its inputs: +26.8 W), and its own JT-stage warm-end term implies 5.91 g/s through the JT valve against 5.66 g/s printed. Closing the balance by solving only the warm turbine inlet (88.95 K; cold turbine inlet and 7 bar kept from the sheet) gives 13.49 g/s. That is a closed point, not an optimum: the cold turbine inlet and the intermediate pressure were not searched, so it does not by itself confirm the book's conclusion that the parallel arrangement is better.
  • LN2 duty: the book's HX0 boundary has a 0.22 K gap. Figs. 3.60 and 3.61 return the cold-box LP at 79.00 K but start HX0 at 78.78 K. The model uses one boundary (79.00 K), giving 119.9 W (Fig. 3.60, book 108.41 W) and 1237.9 W (Fig. 3.61, book 1228.94 W). With the book's 78.78 K the model gives 108.5 W and 1229.1 W, so the gap, not the model, explains the difference. Nitrogen: 427.0 J/g to vaporise at 1.2 bar and warm to 297 K.

Model

JT envelope: ṁJT·hHP(T3) + Q = L·hl,sat + (ṁJT − L)·hLP(T7), with T3 = T7 + 0.2 K.
Brayton envelope: turbine inlet solved so the η = 0.75 expansion ends at T7; ṁt from the cold-box energy balance with a 3 K warm-end difference.
Optimum: golden-section minimum of the compressor (LP return) flow. Isothermal power: ṁHP·(R/M)·T·ln(PH/PL).
